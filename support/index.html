Help and support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Answers to common questions are below.

Contact: email drwood1012@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and what you were doing, and a screenshot if you can. Beta testers can also take a screenshot in the app and send it through TestFlight.

Your data

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone, inside QuietCore. You don't sign in and your progress isn't uploaded. If you turn on Friends, only your hero's name and look, your friends list and gifts are kept on our server. Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) include your progress.

How do I back up, or move to a new phone?

Go to Settings → Backup & restore → Save backup file and choose where it goes: Save to Files (iCloud Drive works well), AirDrop or email. On the new phone, open the same screen, tap Open backup file and pick it. Restoring an iPhone from an iCloud backup also brings QuietCore's data along.

How do I start over?

Settings → Delete all data erases everything on this device. Save a backup first if you might want it back.

Friends

How do I add a friend?

Go to Hero → Friends and turn friends on. Send your friend code to someone (Copy or Share), or type theirs into A friend's code and tap Add. They'll see your request the next time they open QuietCore, and you're friends once they accept.

How do gifts and streaks work?

You can send each friend one gift a day and open up to 20 a day. A gift has the same odds as your daily gift. Your streak with a friend grows by one for each day you both send, and ends if a day is missed. An hourglass means it ends tonight unless you both send.

Someone is bothering me, or has an offensive name

Tap them in your friends list or requests, then Report or Block. Blocking removes them and stops them sending you requests or gifts; they aren't told. Reports are reviewed within a day. You can also email us.

How do I delete my hero from the server?

Hero → Friends → Turn off friends and delete my hero from the server. Your friends list, streaks and unopened gifts are deleted straight away. The rest of the app is unaffected. Settings → Delete all data also removes it.

I got a new phone. Do I keep my friends?

Yes, if you move with a backup file (Settings → Backup & restore) or restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup. The backup carries the key to your hero. If you set up fresh without one, turn friends on again and re-add your friends.

Apple Health

How do I connect it?

Tap the Connect Apple Health card on the Daily tab, or go to Settings → Apple Health. iPhone will ask which data QuietCore may read. Turn on Steps, Sleep, Water and Workouts, then tap Allow.

My steps or sleep aren't showing up
  • Check access: iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → QuietCore, and make sure the categories are on.
  • Open the Health app and confirm the data is there (for example under Browse → Sleep).
  • In QuietCore, go to Settings → Apple Health and tap Sync now.
  • Only quests linked to Health fill in by themselves. On the Apple Health page, tap Link next to a suggested quest, or edit a quest and choose Fill in from Apple Health.
Why doesn't my sleep match my sleep app exactly?

QuietCore counts time asleep for the night that ends on a given morning (6 pm to 6 pm). If your Watch and a sleep app both record the same night, overlapping time is counted once instead of being added together. Some apps count time in bed rather than time asleep, which can make their number a little higher.

Which workouts come in from Health?

Runs and rides (they count toward your running plan), plus walks, hikes, yoga and stretching as recovery sessions. Strength workouts aren't imported, because lifts are logged set by set in QuietCore and would count twice. A workout you already logged by hand is skipped.

Quests and XP

Why did a quest stop giving XP today?

Each stat can earn up to 40 XP a day from quests (more with buffs, up to a ceiling). This keeps progress honest, so a long list of easy quests can't race you to level 100. Your streak still counts after you reach the limit.

Why are my stats going down?

Like real fitness, stats slowly fade if you go a long stretch without training them. Rest days are fine; a short break won't hurt. If you're sick or injured, recovery mode pauses the fade for a while.

Can I undo a quest I checked by mistake?

Yes. Tap the checked box again (or the undo button right after). For amount quests like water, open the quest's log and use Take back.

Reminders

I'm not getting reminders

In QuietCore, go to Settings → Reminders and make sure they're on. Then check iPhone Settings → Notifications → QuietCore and allow notifications. Focus modes can also hold them back.
