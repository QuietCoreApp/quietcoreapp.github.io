Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

The short version: QuietCore doesn't collect your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter, and everything QuietCore reads from Apple Health or your calendar, stays on your iPhone. The only things that leave it are lookups: food you ask about (a barcode, a search, or a meal you describe for an estimate), and the weather for an outdoor run that didn't come with it (an approximate place and a time). They're sent without your name or any account, just to get the answer back.

QuietCore is an iPhone app made by Daniel Wood, an independent developer ("we" and "us" below). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What QuietCore stores

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • The hero name and look you choose
  • Your quests, what you've checked off and logged, and your streaks
  • Workouts, runs, rides and recovery sessions you log, including weights, distances and times
  • Training programs, running plans, planner events and body check-ins you enter
  • Journal entries, if you turn on the optional journal
  • Game progress (levels, coins, pets, items, your base) and your settings
  • Steps, sleep, water, workouts, heart rate, VO2 max, body weight, body fat and run routes from Apple Health, if you connect it (see below)
  • Foods you log, and heart rate zone or test results you enter
  • The age, sex, height, weight and activity level you enter in a nutrition guide's calculator, and the health conditions you tick when starting a program (used only to adjust that program)

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us. We can't see it, and we don't have a copy. The app connects to the internet only for the food and weather lookups described below and to check whether a newer version of the app is available (that check downloads a small file and sends nothing about you).

Like other app data, it's included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have those turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple and protected by your Apple Account. They aren't accessible to us.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you connect it, QuietCore asks permission to read these categories, and you choose which to allow:

  • Steps
  • Sleep (time asleep and in bed, and sleep stages if your Apple Watch records them)
  • Water
  • Workouts (type, start and end time, duration, distance, calories, and pool length and laps for swims)
  • Heart rate during workouts and overnight, resting heart rate and VO2 max
  • Body weight and body fat percentage
  • Workout routes (the GPS path of a run or ride)

QuietCore uses this data only to fill in your quests (for example, a steps or sleep goal), to log your workouts inside the app, and to show your progress (heart rate zones, VO2 max trends, your weight trend, sleep insights such as your stages, wake-ups and overnight heart rate dip, calories burned by exercise, and a map of a run that only you can see). Specifically:

  • QuietCore never writes to or changes anything in Apple Health.
  • Health data is processed and kept only on your iPhone. It is never uploaded, never sent to us, and never stored in iCloud by QuietCore.
  • Health data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or marketing, or used for anything other than the features above.

You can change or turn off QuietCore's access at any time in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → QuietCore, or tap Disconnect Apple Health in the app's settings.

Calendar

Linking your iPhone calendar is optional. If you link it, QuietCore reads your events to show them in the app's Planner, and adds the workouts and Planner events you schedule to the calendar you pick. Calendar data stays on your iPhone; it is never uploaded or sent to us. Turning the link off removes QuietCore's upcoming events from your calendar. You can also change access in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Sharing

When you tap Share on a streak, achievement, workout or run, the app draws a picture on your iPhone and opens the iPhone share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app or contact there, and only that picture is shared. A run's picture can show the shape of its route, never a map, street names or place names. By default the first and last quarter mile are left off so it doesn't show where you started or finished, and you can leave the route off entirely.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap Scan barcode or Snap a meal.

  • Scan barcode reads a food barcode. No photo or video is saved or sent; only the barcode number is used, for the lookup below.
  • Snap a meal uses a photo you take or pick from your library for one AI meal estimate (below). The app shrinks the photo and sends only that one picture. QuietCore can't see the rest of your photo library, and the photo isn't saved in the app or on our server.

Food lookups and AI meal estimates

When you look up a food, QuietCore sends only what's needed to get its nutrition back:

  • Barcodes and food searches (the barcode number or the words you typed) go to the public food databases Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central, directly or through our food server.
  • AI meal estimates (a Plus feature): the meal you describe and the restaurant name, if you add one, go to our food server, which asks Anthropic's Claude model to estimate the nutrition, using web searches for the restaurant's published nutrition. If you add a photo of your meal, the photo is sent the same way, used only for that estimate and not stored on our server. Anthropic processes the text and photo to produce the estimate under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models.
  • Our food server runs on Cloudflare. It caches database results so repeat lookups are fast, and to keep AI costs in check it counts estimates per day using a random ID created by the app (not linked to your name, Apple Account or device identifiers). Those counts expire after two days. We don't keep the meals you describe or the photos you send.
  • Nothing from Apple Health, your calendar, your food log or the rest of your QuietCore data is ever included.

Weather for your runs

Heat, humidity and daylight change heart rate and effort, so QuietCore records the conditions for outdoor runs and rides. Runs from an Apple Watch already include the weather. For other outdoor runs, QuietCore asks Apple's weather service (Apple Weather / WeatherKit) for the weather at the time of the run, once per run:

  • What's sent: a location rounded to about half a mile, and the run's start and end time. Nothing that identifies you is included.
  • The location is the start of the run's GPS route when it has one, or the town you set as your running area in the app's settings (kept on your phone, also rounded). QuietCore doesn't use your phone's location services for this.
  • Apple returns the weather, and QuietCore saves it with the run on your phone. Indoor and treadmill runs are never looked up.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, QuietCore schedules them on your iPhone using Apple's local notifications. No push server is involved and nothing is sent off your device.

Backup files

You can export a backup file of your progress from the app's settings. The file is created on your device and saved wherever you choose. We never receive it. Keep it somewhere safe, because it contains your quest and workout history.

Purchases

If QuietCore offers in-app purchases, they're processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app only learns which items you own, so it can unlock them.

Beta testing (TestFlight)

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share some information with us under the TestFlight terms: the email address or name you were invited with, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use it only to fix problems and improve QuietCore.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we'll receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix the issue. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Children

QuietCore isn't directed at children under 13. Because the app doesn't collect personal information, we don't knowingly collect any from children.

Deleting your data

Because everything is on your device, you're in control:

  • In the app, go to Settings → Delete all data to erase everything QuietCore has saved.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone also deletes its data.
  • Disconnecting Apple Health stops new reading right away. iOS permissions can be removed in the Health settings shown above.

Health information disclaimer

QuietCore's facts, programs and suggestions are general fitness and wellness information, not medical advice. Talk with a qualified health professional before starting a new exercise or nutrition program, especially if you have a health condition or an injury.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a future version of QuietCore ever needs to collect information (for example, to add friends), we'll update this policy first, explain what's collected and why, and ask for your permission in the app where required.
